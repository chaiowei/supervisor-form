Privacy Policy / 隱私權政策

This page describes how the Daily Work Log form (used internally by our construction supervisors and contractors) handles data.

本頁說明「監工日誌」表單（僅供內部負責工程師與承包商使用）如何處理資料。

What we collect / 收集的資料

Daily work log entries you submit (project, contractor, work details, site photos) and your LINE display name and user ID, used only to deliver the report back to you.

您送出的日誌內容（工程、廠商、施工內容、現場照片），以及 LINE 顯示名稱與使用者 ID，僅用於把報告回傳給您。

How it is used and stored / 用途與儲存

Data is used only to generate the daily report. Reports and photos are stored in the project owner's own Google Drive and Notion workspace. Google account access (Google Drive, Gmail) is used only by our own automation to save these files and send report emails. We do not sell or share data with third parties.

資料僅用於產生監工日誌。報告與照片存放在專案負責人自己的 Google Drive 與 Notion。Google 帳號權限（Google Drive、Gmail）只供我們自己的自動化流程存檔與寄送報告，不會出售或提供給第三方。
